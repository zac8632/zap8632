# Hook Taxonomy — Reference for MEI

MEI produces 8 hook variations per post. This file defines the full hook library MEI selects from.

## English Hooks (15 types, 4 trigger families)

### 🧠 Curiosity family
1. **Information gap** — "Here's what most KL buyers miss about new launches…"
2. **Pattern interrupt** — "5 years in this industry, first time I've seen this."
3. **Insider reveal** — "The one thing developers don't put on the brochure."

### 💥 Emotional family
4. **Contrarian punch** — "Unpopular take: stop registering at every showroom."
5. **Fear-lit** — "The RM50k mistake I see buyers make every month."
6. **Aspiration** — "How a RM200k GCI agent hit RM1M in 18 months."

### 📖 Story family
7. **Story-led** — "A client almost lost RM120k last week. Here's how we caught it."
8. **Confession** — "I made this mistake in year 1. Cost me a friendship."
9. **Character-led** — "Meet the buyer who taught me everything about new launches."

### 🔢 Logic family
10. **Number-led** — "RM3,475/month. That's the number that decides this deal."
11. **List promise** — "5 things to check before signing the SPA."
12. **Framework** — "The 3-question test I run before every project."

### 🎯 Direct engagement family
13. **Objection-led** — "'What if the developer abandons it?' — my honest answer."
14. **Question-led** — "Should you buy a new launch in 2026?"
15. **Command** — "Stop trusting the price list. Do this instead."

## Hook selection rules

- **Educational pillar** → prefer types 10, 11, 12 (Logic family)
- **Contrarian pillar** → prefer types 4, 15, 13 (Emotional + Command + Objection)
- **Personal pillar** → prefer types 7, 8, 9 (Story family)
- **IG carousel** → cover slide works best with types 4, 10, 15
- **Reel opener** → works best with types 2, 5, 7, 14
- **LinkedIn** → works best with types 6, 12, 14 (professional-lean)
- **WhatsApp broadcast** → works best with types 11, 13 (utility-lean)

## Anti-patterns (never use)

- "Hot property!" / "Don't miss out!" / "Once in a lifetime!" / "Limited units!" / "DM me now!"
- Fake urgency ("Only 3 units left!!!" without proof)
- Vague superlatives ("Amazing project!", "Great investment!")
