# YouTube Ideation

YouTube ideas built for how YouTube actually surfaces content — search intent, browse appeal, and authority-building. Not generic content ideas.

## When to run this

- Planning the next month of YouTube
- Stuck for video ideas that will actually get discovered
- Building a content calendar around search + browse
- Finding authority-building video topics in the niche

## What makes a YouTube idea different

YouTube ideas need: a search or browse reason to exist, a title that telegraphs the value, and a clear viewer intent. This skill generates ideas with all three — not just topics.

## Inputs

Required:
- The niche/pillars (read from pillar doc if available)

Reads if available:
- Voice doc, audience insight, channel's top performers
- Recent videos (to avoid repetition)

## Output structure

```
# YouTube Ideas — [date]

## The ideas

### 1. [Working title direction]
**Intent:** [Search / Browse / Both]
**Why it gets discovered:** [The search term or browse appeal behind it]
**The promise:** [What the viewer gets]
**Pillar:** [Which pillar]
**Format:** [Tutorial / breakdown / story / list / case study]
**Hook direction:** [Climax-led cold open angle]

### 2. ...
[Continue for 10 ideas]

## The search plays
[Which ideas target search traffic — evergreen, ranks over time]

## The browse plays
[Which ideas target browse/suggested — spikes fast, rides momentum]

## Make this next
[The strongest idea and why]
```

## Process

1. Read pillars, audience, top performers
2. Generate 10 ideas — each with intent, discovery reason, promise, format, hook direction
3. Split into search plays vs browse plays
4. Name the one to make next

## Critical rules

- Every idea needs a discovery reason — why would YouTube surface this? If there's no search or browse logic, it's not a YouTube idea.
- Title directions, not finished titles (YouTube Packaging finishes them).
- Separate search plays (evergreen) from browse plays (momentum) — they're different strategies.
- Tie ideas to pillars and authority — random viral ideas that don't build the brand are noise.
- Avoid recent topics unless there's a genuinely fresh angle.
